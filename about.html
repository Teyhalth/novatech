WHAT IS AI or ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENT?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding information, recognizing patterns, generating content, solving problems, and assisting with decisions.

From studying and researching to coding and creating, AI can help people accomplish tasks faster and explore new ideas. However, AI is a tool—not a replacement for human judgment. Always review AI-generated information and use it responsibly.
